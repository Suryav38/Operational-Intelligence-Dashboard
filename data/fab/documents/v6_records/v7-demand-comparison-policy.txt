Demand comparison policy (updated for v10) · SYNTHETIC
History ends with reporting quarter 2026Q3. 2026Q4 is in progress and has no actuals.
The 2026Q4 values are a trend forecast: for each fab and program, the 2026Q3 value multiplied by one plus its mean quarter-on-quarter change over the last four quarters, computed separately for requested starts and actual starts. Totals: 2,994 requested and 2,789 starts per week.
The Q4 forecast is not used to fit or backtest the realization model.
2027 requests are stated on the same basis as historical requests (planned run rate divided by each program's realization factor), so the request and expected-start series continue from 2026 without a step at the year boundary.
